# 0002: Religious depiction and celebrations

Status: decided by the owners, 5 Nov 2026.

The owners have reviewed how dress, titles and religious gestures appear in the game (kufi, imama, beards, sheikh,
mufti, hafiz; the sujood and du'a taunts), so no separate community review is needed (CD8). Celebrations may include
religious gestures as long as they are respectful and never mocking: used after a player's own win or big play,
never aimed at an opponent, and animated faithfully (for sujood: forehead, nose, palms, knees and toes on the
ground, at an unhurried pace), never in a way that looks funny or odd.
