# 0002: Religious depiction and celebrations

Status: decided by the owners, 5–6 Nov 2026.

The owners have reviewed how dress, titles and religious gestures appear in the game (kufi, imama, beards, sheikh,
mufti, hafiz; the du'a and takbir taunts), so no separate community review is needed (CD8). Celebrations may include
religious gestures as long as they are respectful and never mocking: used after a player's own win or big play,
never aimed at an opponent, and animated faithfully, never in a way that looks funny or odd.

Sujood is not used anywhere in the game (6 Nov 2026): the taunt and the profile celebration were removed in 9.17.0,
and no celebration should add it back.

Faces show expressions with the eyes and brows only; there are no mouths (6 Nov 2026), since a beard covers the mouth
and drawn mouths over beards didn't read well.
