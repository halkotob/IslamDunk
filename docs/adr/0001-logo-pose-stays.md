# 0001: The logo's dunker pose stays

Status: decided by the owners, 5 Nov 2026.

The design review (VD1) noted that the logo's dunker pose (`src/render/logo.js`) could read like a well-known
sportswear silhouette at small sizes. The owners keep the pose as it is: it's a dunker in a thawb and kufi, our own
mark. Motion and polish may be added to it (VD3), but future reviews shouldn't propose changing the pose.
